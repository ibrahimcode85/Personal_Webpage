Malaysia Apnea Warrior 5.0
18 October 2026
Competed in the fifth edition of Malaysia Apnea Warrior (MAW5) in Kuala Lumpur, with my main focus this time on Dynamic With Fins (DYN). Coming into the competition, I had been working towards improving my consistency and building the confidence to push beyond my previous best distance. My final training session had not gone quite as well as I had hoped, so I was a little uncertain about how I would perform on competition day. Fortunately, things came together, and I managed to complete a clean 163m dive, setting a new personal best and improving on my previous record of 153m from the World Championship in Budapest. It was particularly satisfying to see the progress after months of training, especially in a discipline where I am still developing my technique and confidence. Although there is still some distance to go before reaching my longer-term goals, I was happy with the performance and grateful to see another small step forward in my freediving journey. A good reminder to be patient with the process and appreciate the progress along the way.
