AIDA Merdeka Freediver Team Battle
30 Aug 2026
Competed in the AIDA Merdeka Freediver Team Battle held at The Campus Ampang in Kuala Lumpur over two days, with the first day dedicated to individual events and the second day to the team competition. It was particularly nice to compete in my usual training pool, where I had also participated in my very first freediving competition. The first day was rather challenging, with the afternoon heat and an unusually late start making it difficult to settle into my usual pre-dive routine. Despite this, I managed a clean 103m dive in Dynamic No Fins (DNF), finishing first overall in the discipline. The following day was a more comfortable experience, with an earlier start and a better opportunity to prepare for my Dynamic Bi-Fins (DYNB) dive. I managed to reach 150m, a distance I was quite happy with, although I unfortunately received a red card for an unsuccessful surface protocol. It was particularly disappointing knowing that my result would not count towards the team's overall score. Nevertheless, I was grateful for the opportunity to compete alongside my teammates and gain more experience. Looking back, the competition was a useful reminder of how much the little details — from preparation and nutrition to managing energy and staying composed — can influence performance. Another valuable experience in my freediving journey, with a few lessons to carry forward into future competitions.
